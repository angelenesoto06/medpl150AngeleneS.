About Me!

I am Angelene and I am 19 years old. I want to major in Psychology but I would Like to work with children as a children's psychologist! While watching animated films, I love the color changes in scenes that animators doI would like to explore some animated flims and see why certain scenes are colored differently and the feelings those scenes invoke. I think many feel that a movies soundtrack invokes the most feeling however, I disagree. When a scene uses different colors or settings than the rest, it automatically make us wonder why there might be a change. I am excited to learn about different forms of media, like short flims and maybe journalism, and I cannot wait to see the artwork of my peers as well.
